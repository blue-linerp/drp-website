category: Getting in
question: How do I get whitelisted?
---
Sign in on the [Applications](/applications) page with Discord, make sure you're in our Discord server, and fill out the **Whitelist** application. Once it's accepted, the Whitelisted role is added in Discord automatically and you can connect from the [Play](/queue) page.

---
category: Getting in
question: How long do applications take?
---
Most whitelist applications get a response within **2–5 days**. Department and staff applications can take longer because they often include an interview. You can check the status any time on your [profile](/profile).

category: Getting in
question: What's the age requirement?
---
The **Whitelist** server is **18+**. The **Public** server is **13+**. Staff can ask you to verify your age, and lying about it leads to removal.

---
category: Getting in
question: What's the difference between the whitelist and public server?
---
The whitelist server has a higher roleplay standard, a reviewed application, and a more serious, story-driven community. The public server is open to anyone over 13 and is a good place to learn the ropes.

category: Playing
question: How does the queue work?
---
When the city is full, players wait in a queue. Your place depends on your **queue priority** first, and then on when you joined. If you crash, you keep your spot for **3 minutes** while you reconnect.

---
category: Playing
question: What does queue priority do?
---
Priority moves you ahead of players with lower priority in the queue. It never skips the whitelist, and it doesn't affect anything else in the city. Your priority breakdown is on your [profile](/profile) and the [Play](/queue) page.

---
category: Playing
question: Can I have multiple characters?
---
Yes. Each character must be clearly different, and your characters can't share money, items or information. Gang rules limit you to one gang across all characters.

---
category: Playing
question: I can't connect. What do I do?
---
Make sure Discord is open **before** you start FiveM, then restart FiveM. Check that you're whitelisted on the [Play](/queue) page. If it still doesn't work, follow [Connecting & Troubleshooting](/guides/connecting-troubleshooting) or open a support ticket.

---
category: Community
question: How do I report a player?
---
Open a **Report a player** ticket in our Discord. Include the time, the names or IDs involved, and a clip if you have one. Please don't report players in public channels.

---
category: Community
question: How do I appeal a ban?
---
Open a **Ban appeal** ticket in our Discord. Explain what happened honestly and what you'd do differently. Appeals are reviewed by a different staff member than the one who issued the ban.

---
category: Community
question: Can I stream on the server?
---
Yes, and we'd love you to. Put **{{SHORT_NAME}}** in your stream title to show up on the [Creators](/creators) page. Want more? Apply through the **Content Creator** application.

category: Supporting
question: How do supporter tiers work?
---
Supporter tiers are monthly subscriptions through our store. Each tier gives a Discord role, a few cosmetic perks and extra queue priority. Supporting never bypasses the whitelist or the rules.

---
category: Supporting
question: Can I get a refund?
---
Supporter perks are digital and start right away, so purchases are generally non-refundable. You can cancel a subscription at any time to stop future payments. Read the full [Refund Policy](/refund).

Supporter tiers on {{SERVER_NAME}} are digital goods sold through our store, powered by [Tebex](https://www.tebex.io/). This policy works alongside Tebex's own checkout terms.

## Digital perks start right away

When you buy a supporter tier, your perks (Discord role, queue priority and cosmetics) are applied automatically, usually within a few minutes. Because the perks are delivered right away, **purchases are generally non-refundable**.

## Subscriptions

- Supporter tiers renew monthly until you cancel.
- You can cancel at any time from your Tebex or PayPal account. Your perks stay active until the end of the period you've already paid for.
- We don't offer partial refunds for unused time.

## When we will refund

- You were charged twice for the same purchase.
- Your perks weren't delivered and we couldn't fix it within 7 days.
- A refund is required by consumer protection law where you live.

To request one, open a **Purchase / refund issue** ticket on the [Support](/support) page with your Tebex transaction ID.

## Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback instead of contacting us first automatically removes your supporter perks, and can lead to a ban from the store and the server.

## Bans

If you're banned for breaking the rules, you lose access to your perks and aren't entitled to a refund.

## Monetisation rules

We follow the [Cfx.re / FiveM monetisation guidelines](https://fivem.net/terms). Perks never give a gameplay advantage that bypasses the whitelist or the rules.

This policy explains what {{SERVER_NAME}} ("we", "us") collects when you use this website and our FiveM server, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**From Discord, when you sign in**
- Your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar.
- Your account creation date, worked out from your Discord ID.
- Your membership in our Discord server: your join date and roles.

We request the `identify`, `guilds` and `guilds.members.read` scopes. We **don't** read your messages, friends list or email address. Your Discord access token is used only during sign-in and is not stored.

**From the FiveM server, when you connect**
- Your FiveM licence identifier, Steam identifier (if present), Cfx.re account ID (if present), and the IP address you connected from.
- When you were last seen in game, and your total playtime.

**From Steam and Cfx.re, if you link them on your profile**
- Your SteamID (stored as the `steam:` identifier FiveM uses) and, if available, your Steam display name. Steam sign-in only confirms which account you own. We never see your Steam password.
- Your Cfx.re forum user ID (stored as the `fivem:` identifier) and username. We request a single-use, read-only key to read them, then revoke it immediately.
- You can unlink either account at any time from your profile.

**From you**
- Answers you give in applications, and your settings (DM notifications, profile visibility).

**From our store (Tebex)**
- Which supporter tier you bought and when it renews or ends. Payment details are handled entirely by Tebex. We never see your card details.

**Records we create**
- Application decisions, staff notes, warnings, bans, and an audit log of staff actions.

## Why we use it

- To check your whitelist access and queue priority, and to let you into the server.
- To review applications and keep the community safe (bans, appeals, abuse prevention).
- To give you supporter perks and Discord roles.
- To show you your own profile, application history and playtime.

## Who can see it

Staff can see your profile, applications and moderation history. Department heads can see applications for their department only. If you make your profile public, other visitors can see your name, avatar, badges and creator links. We don't sell your data.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account while it's active. Moderation records (bans and warnings) may be kept after you delete your account, to enforce bans. Audit logs are kept for up to 2 years.

## Your choices

- Turn Discord DM notifications on or off on your [profile](/profile).
- **Delete your account** from your profile. This removes your account, applications and linked identifiers, except active ban records, which we keep to enforce the ban.
- Ask for a copy of your data, or a correction, through a support ticket.

## Cookies

We use one essential, secure session cookie to keep you signed in. We also store small preferences in your browser, like dismissed announcements and application drafts. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

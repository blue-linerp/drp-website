## Starter jobs

Head to the **Job Center** in the city. These jobs don't need an application:

- **Delivery driver**: learn the map and earn steady pay.
- **Tow truck**: help clear wrecks and meet a lot of people.
- **Taxi**: roleplay with everyone who gets in your car.
- **Garbage collector** and **miner**: quiet, reliable income.

## Whitelisted jobs

Some careers need an application through the website:

- **Police**, **EMS** and **DOJ**: see [Joining a Department](/guides/joining-a-department).
- **Business ownership**: apply through the **Business** application.

## Working for a player business

Players run restaurants, mechanic shops and more. Walk in, ask if they're hiring, and roleplay an interview. These jobs often pay better than starter jobs and lead to the best stories.

> [!TIP]
> Ask your boss for a few shifts before you ask for a raise. Reputation in the city is everything.

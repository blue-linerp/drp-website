## Buying a vehicle

Visit the **dealership**. Player salespeople can give test drives and better deals. You'll need a driver's licence from City Hall first.

## Garages

Park at any public garage to store your vehicle. If you leave a vehicle out in the city, it can be towed to the **impound**, and getting it back costs a fee.

- Press `kbd:E` at a garage marker to store or take out a vehicle.
- Lock your car with `kbd:L`. Unlocked cars are fair game.

## Fuel and repairs

Fill up at gas stations, and watch the fuel arc on your speedometer. For damage, visit a player-run **mechanic shop**. Repair kits only get you to the shop.

> [!TIP]
> Buckle up with `kbd:B`. Crashing at speed without a seatbelt can throw you through the windshield.

## Connecting

1. Install [FiveM](https://fivem.net/) and launch GTA V through it at least once.
2. Open **Discord** before FiveM. We use your Discord account to check your whitelist.
3. Go to the [Play](/queue) page, sign in, and press **Connect**. You can also press `kbd:F8` in FiveM and paste the `connect` command shown there.

## "Open Discord and restart FiveM"

FiveM couldn't see your Discord account. Fully close FiveM, make sure Discord's desktop app is running (not the browser version), then start FiveM again.

## "Not whitelisted"

Your whitelist application hasn't been accepted yet, or your Discord role is missing. Check the [Play](/queue) page, which shows exactly what's wrong. If it says you're whitelisted but you still can't join, use **Re-check** and try again in a minute.

## Stuck loading or crashing

- Clear your FiveM cache: delete everything in `FiveM Application Data/data/` **except** the `game-storage` folder.
- Update your graphics drivers.
- Turn off overlays (Discord overlay, MSI Afterburner, GeForce Experience) as a test.
- Close any mod menus or injectors. They'll get you banned anyway.

> [!TIP]
> Crashed mid-queue? You keep your spot for **3 minutes**, so just reconnect.

## Still stuck?

Open a **Bug report** ticket on the [Support](/support) page with a screenshot of the error and your `CitizenFX.log`.

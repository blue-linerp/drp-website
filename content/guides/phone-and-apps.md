Open your phone with `kbd:M`.

## Core apps

- **Contacts & Messages**: save numbers and text people in character.
- **Bank**: check your balance and send money to people you're standing near.
- **Twitter**: the city's social feed. Supporters can get a verified badge.
- **Garage**: see where your vehicles are.
- **Services**: call police, EMS, mechanics or taxis.

> [!WARNING]
> Everything on your phone is in character. Don't post OOC information on in-game social media.

## Losing your phone

Phones can be taken in robberies or lost if you're arrested. Buy a new one at any convenience store. Your number and contacts stay with your character.

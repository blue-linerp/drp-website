## Who can apply

You must be **whitelisted** first. Departments look for players who've spent some time in the city and know the rules well.

## The process

1. **Apply** through the department's application on the [Applications](/applications) page.
2. **Review**: the department head reads your application. This usually takes up to a week.
3. **Interview**: if your application stands out, you'll be invited to a voice interview in Discord.
4. **Academy**: new hires go through training before their first patrol or shift.

## What makes a strong application

- Answers written in your own words, with real thought put into them.
- An understanding of why the department matters to the city's stories.
- Patience and good judgement in the scenarios.

> [!WARNING]
> Using AI to write applications is prohibited and leads to an automatic denial.

## After you're accepted

The department role is added to your Discord account automatically, and you'll get access to the department's channels and SOPs.

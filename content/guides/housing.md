## Starter apartment

Every new character gets a small apartment. It has a stash and a wardrobe, and it's where you'll spawn after logging in.

## Renting and buying

Talk to a **real estate agent** (a player job) to view properties. Agents help you find a place that fits your budget and handle the paperwork in character.

- **Rentals** are charged weekly from your bank account.
- **Purchased homes** can be furnished, and you can give keys to other characters.

> [!WARNING]
> If you miss rent payments, you can lose access to your property, along with anything you left inside it.

## Storage

Houses have a stash whose size depends on the property. Keep valuables in your house stash, not in your car.

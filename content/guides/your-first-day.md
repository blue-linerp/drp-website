Welcome to {{SHORT_NAME}}. Everyone starts in the same place: a bus stop, a little cash, and a city full of people you haven't met yet.

## Before you spawn

Make your character someone you'd enjoy playing for months. Give them a name, an age, and one reason they came to the city. You don't need a full backstory. Most of the best characters are discovered through play.

> [!TIP]
> Keep a short note of your character's name, birthday and job. Police and EMS will ask, and staying consistent makes scenes smoother.

## Your first hour

1. **Get your ID** at City Hall. Most jobs and the bank need it.
2. **Open a bank account** so you're not carrying all your money.
3. **Pick up a starter job** at the Job Center. Delivery, towing and taxi are good ways to learn the map.
4. **Grab food and water.** Your hunger and thirst bars are bottom-left on the HUD.
5. **Talk to people.** Ask for directions, ask what's good to eat, ask who's hiring.

## Useful basics

- Open your phone with `kbd:M`.
- Open your inventory with `kbd:Tab`.
- Use `/me` to describe actions the game can't show, and keep it short.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't spend your starter money on a car right away. A bike or the bus gets you around fine until you've got a job lined up.

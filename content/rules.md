category: GENERAL
title: Server Philosophy
points: Staff Discretion
---
**Roleplay first.** Every rule on this page exists to protect good stories. When a situation isn't covered here, ask yourself what makes the scene better for *everyone* involved, not just for you, and do that.

- The **Whitelist** server is for players aged **18 and over**. The **Public** server is open to players aged **13 and over**.
- Staff can act on behaviour that clearly goes against the spirit of these rules, even if a rule doesn't name it exactly.
- Not knowing the rules doesn't excuse breaking them. Updates are posted in Discord and in the changelog at the bottom of this page.

---
category: GENERAL
title: General Rules
points: 10–100 Points
---
These apply everywhere: in the city, in Discord, in voice, and in tickets.

- **Be mature.** Treat other players and staff with respect, both in character and out of it.
- **No hate speech or discrimination.** Slurs, or targeting someone for race, religion, gender, sexuality or disability, are never acceptable, in character or not.
- **No harassment.** Don't follow, target or provoke a player out of character, and don't carry IC conflict into DMs.
- **No backseat moderation.** Report problems through a ticket instead of policing other players yourself.
- **No exploiting.** Using bugs, glitches, mod menus, macros or third-party tools to gain an advantage gets you removed. Report exploits privately.
- **No AI in applications.** Applications must be written by you. Using AI to write them means an automatic denial and can lead to a ban.
- **Sportsmanship.** Win gracefully, lose gracefully. Losing a scene is part of the story.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Roleplay Standards
points: 10–50 Points
---
We follow a **"Yes, and"** approach: accept what other players bring into a scene and build on it instead of shutting it down.

- Stay in character in public spaces at all times.
- Give other players time and space to respond. Scenes aren't races.
- Play a realistic character with realistic motives. Your character can be larger than life, but they should still feel like a person.
- Let scenes breathe. Not every interaction needs a gun, a chase or a payout.

category: GENERAL
title: Off-Server Conduct
points: Staff Discretion
---
How you act outside the city still matters. Doxxing, organising harassment, leaking private community information, or seriously harming the community on other platforms can lead to removal from both the server and the Discord.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Content Restrictions
points: 20–100 Points
---
Some topics are off-limits in every scene: sexual content involving minors, sexual assault, real-world terrorism and extremist imagery, and suicide roleplay used for shock value. Torture and gore must be **consented to OOC** by everyone involved before the scene starts, and can be faded to black at any time.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Out of Character (OOC)
points: 10–50 Points
---
Keep OOC talk to a minimum while in the city. If you have to break character, use `/ooc` briefly or open a ticket. Don't mention streams, Discord, "the server" or other players' real names in character. Phrases like "my head hurts" for lag or "the city" for the server are accepted.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Criminal Activity
points: 15–70 Points
---
- Any PvP encounter is limited to **4 players per side** unless a specific activity allows more.
- **No bank-transfer robberies.** You can't force someone to transfer money from their bank account.
- **No scamming items of value**, like vehicles, houses or donator items, that a player paid real money or spent weeks earning.
- Kidnapping on-duty government employees needs a clear, roleplay-driven reason, not just to bait a response.
- Wait the posted cooldown between major heists. Cooldowns are server-wide.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Conflict Cooldown
points: 0–100 Points
---
Once a conflict ends (someone is arrested, downed and transported, or the scene is resolved), everyone involved waits **30 minutes** before re-engaging over the same issue. Retaliating right away turns a story into a loop.

---
category: GENERAL
title: RDM (Random Deathmatch)
points: 15–100 Points
---
Don't attack or kill another player without a valid, roleplay-built reason and a clear chance for them to respond. "They looked at me funny" isn't a reason. Escalate through roleplay first.

---
category: GENERAL
title: VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch)
points: 15–100 Points
---
Don't use a vehicle as a weapon without prior roleplay escalation. Ramming someone to end a foot chase, or running people over for fun, is VDM.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Powergaming
points: 10–100 Points
---
Don't force outcomes on other players or use mechanics in unrealistic ways. Examples: forcing an action without giving the other player a chance to respond, using `/me` to declare results ("/me knocks you out"), or pulling items out of nowhere.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Pocket Wiping
points: 0–100 Points
---
You can take what makes sense for the scene, like weapons, phones or the loot from a job. Stripping someone of everything they own "just because" isn't allowed. Money taken in a robbery should be proportionate to the scene.

---
category: GENERAL
title: NVL / Fear RP
points: 10–60 Points
---
Value your life. If you're outnumbered or at gunpoint, act like it: comply, negotiate, or look for a believable way out. Don't draw on three people who already have you covered.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Metagaming
points: 10–60 Points
---
Don't use information your character didn't learn in character. That includes streams, Discord chats, or what you overheard on a friend's call. Names above heads are for OOC identification only.

---
category: GENERAL
title: NITRP (Not In The Roleplay)
points: 20–100 Points
---
If you weren't part of a scene, don't insert yourself in a way that ruins it, for example by driving into an active police scene to cause chaos or sniping into a hostage negotiation you know nothing about.

---
category: GENERAL
title: NLR / Knockdown System
points: 10–60 Points
---
- **Knocked down** (downed but revived on scene): you remember the scene, but you're hurt. Roleplay your injuries.
- **Respawned at the hospital**: you forget the events leading up to your death and can't return to that scene or seek revenge for it.
- Wait at least **15 minutes** before going back to the area of a scene you respawned from.

---
category: GENERAL
title: Scene & Conflict Rules
points: 15–100 Points
---
- **Combat logging** (disconnecting or going AFK to avoid a scene) is treated seriously. If you crash, come back and tell those involved.
- **Respawn abuse**: don't respawn just to escape EMS, police or a scene you're in.
- Don't loot, rob or kill players while they're in a loading or crash state.

---
category: GANGS
title: Gang Rules
points: 15–100 Points
---
- Every gang must register its **colours** with gang staff before claiming territory. Wearing a gang's colours makes you a valid part of that gang's conflicts.
- You can be in **one gang across all your characters**. Your other characters can't help, harm or work with that gang.
- After leaving a gang, wait a **two-week cooldown** before joining another.
- A maximum of **5 members** can take part in any single criminal activity.
- A gang can own a **limited number of businesses**, set by gang staff, to keep the economy fair.
- **Collective punishment**: if members repeatedly break rules, the whole gang can lose territory, privileges or its registration.

---
category: GANGS
title: Turf & Territory
points: 10–50 Points
---
Territory is earned through roleplay, not by sitting on a corner. Turf wars need an IC build-up and approval from gang staff. Hospitals, police stations and City Hall are never turf.

---
category: GOVERNMENT
title: Government Employees
points: 10–60 Points
---
- On-duty police, EMS and DOJ staff must follow their department's SOPs.
- Don't use department resources (vehicles, weapons, databases) for personal or criminal reasons.
- Corruption storylines need approval from the department head **and** staff before they start.
- EMS are neutral. Don't target EMS who are treating a patient unless there's a strong roleplay reason.

---
category: GOVERNMENT
title: Events
points: Staff Discretion
---
Server events have their own rules, announced beforehand. Joining an event means you agree to those rules. Don't disrupt events you're not part of.

---
category: BUSINESSES
title: Businesses
points: 10–50 Points
---
- Businesses must stay active. One inactive for **14 days** can be reclaimed.
- Set prices within the ranges the economy team provides.
- Employees must be hired through roleplay. No sharing stock with friends for free to drain the economy.
- One business owned per player, unless staff approves otherwise.

---
category: BUSINESSES
title: Character Appearance
points: 10–40 Points
---
Characters must look like realistic people. No invisible or broken clothing, no clipping exploits to hide, and no outfits copied to impersonate a specific player or staff member. Police and EMS uniforms are for members of those departments only.

---
category: DISCORD
title: Discord Rules
points: 10–100 Points
---
- Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
- Keep channels on topic. Use tickets for support, appeals and reports.
- No advertising other servers or communities.
- No spam, mass pings, or NSFW content outside of opt-in channels (if any).
- Don't DM staff for support. Open a ticket so the whole team can help.
